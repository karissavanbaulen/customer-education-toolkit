# OKRs for a customer education team

## Where I used this

At Hotjar, the objective I kept every quarter was "Establish customer education as a necessary tool for revenue growth and lifetime customers." Every key result tied education to something Sales, Success or Finance already measured. That's what got education treated as a growth team, not a cost centre. I later used the same structure for an internal training team, where the goals were about reviewer accuracy and satisfaction instead of revenue.

## How it works

- One objective that says why education exists, kept for the whole year.
- Annual key results, broken into quarterly phases: research and baseline, then build, then monitor and iterate.
- Quarterly key results for one-off launches (a new LMS, a certification).
- Write "X%" until you have a baseline. Set the real number once you've measured it. Don't make one up.

---

## Objective: {Establish customer education as a necessary tool for revenue growth and lifetime customers}

### Annual key results

**KR1: Help Sales close faster with self-serve content.** Include education content in {X}% of closed deals by the end of {year}.

- Q1, research and baseline: identify {8–10} problem areas in the sales and support flow. Get a baseline of sales and support touches per lead from Sales Ops.
- Q2, build: create content for those problem areas and embed it in the self-serve flow. Give Sales a way to send tailored content to each lead.
- Q3–Q4, monitor and iterate: reduce sales and support touches by {X}%. Talk to leads about their experience and adjust.

**KR2: Self-serve onboarding for {segment} customers.** Get {X}% of qualified {segment} leads to engage with onboarding content (webinar, learning path, videos, guides).

- Q1, research & build: plan {4-6} onboarding paths based on what comes up in Sales and Success calls.
- Q2, baseline & measure: publish the paths. Measure how many {segment} customers use them and their new-business MRR.
- Q3–Q4, iterate: get {X} customers through onboarding without a CSM. Contribute {$X} to new-business MRR.

### Quarterly key results

**KR3: Launch {LMS / certification}.** {50} learners complete a full learning path by the end of Q{n}.

**KR4: Build a customer maturity model** to prioritise content that drives retention and expansion.

**KR5: Make the help center ready for localization.** Machine translation set up for every target language.

## Variant: internal training team

**Objective: Fully educated {team / agents} on {upcoming change}.**

- KR1: Courses created for at least {X}% of {team function} current needs.
- KR2: Collect feedback from at least {X}% of learners, with an average satisfaction of {4/5} or higher.
- KR3: Ship at least {5} learner-suggested improvements to the KB and {5} to the LMS.
- KR4: {X}% of help center content audited and up to date by the end of the quarter.
